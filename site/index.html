How it works

A fleet is a machine that turns parking spaces into income.

STEP 1

Reserve the cars

Tesla opened a fleet-purchase and mobility-hub interest form on September 3, 2026. Clutch is applying for 30 Cybercabs and for hub partnership, in tranches of 10.

STEP 2

Build the depot

Fenced lot, DC fast chargers, cleaning bay, cameras. Clutch Industries builds it. Owning the depot cuts the two biggest costs — parking and charging — by more than half.

STEP 3

Put them on the network

Cars run on Tesla's robotaxi app. Tesla dispatches, collects fares and takes its share; the fleet keeps the rest. Two local staff keep 30 cars clean, charged and rolling.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I actually buy a Cybercab?

Not at a dealership. Tesla is taking interest from businesses that want to buy fleets and from partners who can provide hubs and infrastructure. Clutch is applying as both. Individual retail sales have been promised but no date or price is set.

Why not run the cars in Salem?

Oregon still requires a licensed human operator in the vehicle. A bill to allow driverless commercial service died in committee in March 2026. The first 30 cars will run in a Tesla network market — Texas, Florida, or Phoenix — and we'll bring them home when the law changes.

What if fares fall to 50 cents a mile?

Then the business belongs to whoever has the cheapest parking and power. With a rented lot and Supercharging, a 30-car fleet loses money at $0.50. With a Clutch-owned depot, it still covers its loans at about 30% utilization. Move the depot slider in the calculator and watch the break-even fare change.

How is Clutch financing this?

Vehicles on a five-year equipment loan, roughly 17% down, plus a depot budget. Total equity for 30 cars is in the $400k–$450k range. We'll open part of that to outside investors under a proper offering once Tesla confirms terms.

Is this a Tesla company?

No. Clutch Cybercab is an independent fleet operator applying to run vehicles on Tesla's network. Tesla and Cybercab are trademarks of Tesla, Inc.
